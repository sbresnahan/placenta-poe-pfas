Parent-of-origin effects and PFAS exposure in human placenta

2 Genotyping methods

2.1 DNA and arrays

Maternal DNA was extracted from blood collected at mid-gestation, infant DNA from umbilical cord tissue or blood, and paternal DNA from buccal swabs. DNA extraction for mothers and infants was as described previously [1-3]. Isohelix DNA buccal swabs stored at -80 C were equilibrated to room temperature for 1 h; 1 mL of ATL lysis buffer (Qiagen) was added and incubated at room temperature for 30 min, then 100 uL of proteinase K (Qiagen) was added and incubated at 60 C with shaking for 30-60 min. DNA was extracted from the lysates with QIAsymphony DNA kits according to the manufacturer’s instructions.

Paternal buccal-swab DNA (n = 732), maternal buffy-coat DNA collected at 26-28 weeks of gestation (n = 1079) and umbilical-cord DNA from the children (n = 1073) were genotyped on the Illumina OmniExpressExome-8 v1.2 array (~1 million SNPs) [3]. Hybridisation and scanning were performed by Expression Analysis, Inc. (Morrisville, North Carolina). Data were processed with the GenomeStudio Genotyping Module (Illumina): genotypes were called by GenCall (GenTrain clustering, Bayesian calling), and genotypes with a GenCall score < 0.15 were set to missing.

2.2 Array-level QC (before this pipeline)

  • Principal components analysis confirmed self-reported ethnicity.
  • Samples with call rate < 97%, cryptic relatedness, or sex / ethnicity discrepancies were excluded.
  • Alleles are reported on hg19.
  • SNP QC was done separately in the Chinese, Malay and Indian subgroups: a SNP was dropped if call rate < 0.95, MAF < 0.05 or Hardy-Weinberg P < 1e-6.

The resulting PLINK files (one each for infants, mothers and fathers) and the array manifest are the genotype inputs of stage 01.

2.3 Processing in this pipeline

Step What it does
01_genotype_prep/prep_ped.R trio pedigree from the three .fam files
01_genotype_prep/plink2vcf.lsf merge, TOP strand -> hg19 forward strand (bcftools +fixref), REF check
01_genotype_prep/liftover.lsf hg19 -> GRCh38, biallelic SNPs with GRCh38-matching REF
01_genotype_prep/check_mendel.lsf Mendelian error rates
02_ancestry_phasing/infer_ancestry.R PCA + 3-NN ancestry for infants without reported ethnicity
02_ancestry_phasing/split_by_ancestry.lsf per-group filters: missingness <= 0.05, MAF >= 0.05, HWE p >= 1e-6
02_ancestry_phasing/phase.lsf SHAPEIT5 pedigree-aware phasing per group and chromosome
03_imputation_SEAD/ SEAD imputation of phased infants; biallelic, MAF > 0.01, Rsq >= 0.3

See WORKFLOW.md for details.
